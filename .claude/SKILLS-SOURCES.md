## Notes

- The motion skills are the 19 packs listed in [iart-ai/motion-skills](https://github.com/iart-ai/motion-skills),
  which is an index of those repositories and has no skills of its own.
- Two names were taken twice, so two skills were renamed (folder and the `name:` line of its
  `SKILL.md`; nothing else changed): `lower-thirds` from tiktok-video-skills is `tiktok-lower-thirds`,
  and `threejs-animation` from webgl-animation-skills is `webgl-threejs-animation`.
  CloudAI-X's `threejs-animation` and lower-thirds-skills' `lower-thirds` keep their names.
- Network use: only `talking-head` and `talking-head-b-roll` call outside services (fal.ai and
  ElevenLabs, with your own `FAL_KEY` / `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY`, when their scripts are run); a few
  templates load Google Fonts or cdnjs. No skill sends usage data or installs anything by itself.
- Several iart-ai reference files end with a "Built by iart.ai" link; that is promotional text only.
- [zhuyansen/awesome-claude-video-skills](https://github.com/zhuyansen/awesome-claude-video-skills)
  is a curated list of video-skill repositories and contains no skills, so nothing was installed from it.
