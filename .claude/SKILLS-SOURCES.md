# Project skills: where they come from

Eight third-party Claude Code skills, chosen for this repository's work: short vertical ads
for Instagram Reels and TikTok, cut from real footage of the plug-in, with on-screen words
and music. They are copied unchanged from the repositories below; their licences are in
`.claude/skill-licenses/`.

| Skill | Source repository | Commit | Licence | Why it is here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ad-creative-video` | [iart-ai/ad-video-skills](https://github.com/iart-ai/ad-video-skills) | cc80716 | MIT | ad hooks, hook-to-CTA message match, platform specs |
| `video-delivery-specs` | [iart-ai/freelance-motion-skills](https://github.com/iart-ai/freelance-motion-skills) | 9f294eb | MIT | Instagram and other platform specs, export, pre-delivery checks |

## Removed

Installed earlier and then removed as not suited to this kind of ad: the other 48 iart-ai
motion skills (talking heads, slideshows, charts, explainers, web UI animation, WebGL, Manim,
lower thirds, pricing and client work), the 13 three.js skills (CloudAI-X/threejs-skills),
explainroo (voice-over explainers) and Remotion's 14 skills (this repository renders with
HyperFrames). onetake was never installed: its licence allows noncommercial use only.

Some kept skills mention skills that are no longer here (for example `remotion-video`); those
pointers can be ignored.
